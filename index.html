01About Me

My Journey in Business & Finance

I am a BBA student with a growing interest in how businesses operate, how financial systems work and how strategic decisions are made. That curiosity led me to pursue a Bachelor of Business Administration in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore, alongside a BBA in Accounting, Taxation, Auditing at Inspiria Knowledge Campus.

Right now I am building a strong foundation in finance, accounting and business management, and putting it to use through competitions, events and student communities. These experiences are teaching me to approach challenges with structured thinking and a willingness to keep learning.

My long-term goal is to become a competent finance professional who combines analytical discipline, commercial awareness and strategic thinking to contribute meaningfully to an organisation’s growth.

Hometown: Siliguri, West Bengal

A vibrant city in northern West Bengal, known as the gateway to Northeast India and the Himalayas. Siliguri is recognised for its cultural diversity, scenic surroundings, and importance in trade, tourism, and education. It is a city where natural beauty meets urban growth, and I am proud to call it home.

02Leadership & Achievements

Leading from the front row

Compensation, Benefits and Total Rewards – IIM Rohtak
Quiz · Participated

I took part in a quiz on Compensation, Benefits and Total Rewards organised by IIM Rohtak, which was my first look at human resource management. The questions covered salary structures, employee benefits, performance-based incentives, pay equity and reward management.

The quiz showed me how organisations design pay packages to attract, motivate and retain people, and how performance, market standards and employee needs shape those decisions. It also introduced me to motivation theories and their link to employee satisfaction. As a first-year student exploring business, it made me curious about compensation management and organisational behaviour.

FinSight: Sharpen Your Financial Insight – IIM Rohtak
Quiz · Third Position

I secured third position in FinSight: Sharpen Your Financial Insight – 7, an online finance and investment quiz organised by the Finance & Investment Club of IIM Rohtak.

The questions covered market dynamics, portfolio management strategies, investment concepts and financial trends. They asked me to read financial information carefully and apply theory to realistic situations. The result was encouraging, and the quiz also showed me which topics I still need to strengthen. It has pushed me to read more about investment analysis and data-driven financial decisions.

  • Class 11 – Prefect: helped maintain discipline and supported coordination between students and school activities.
  • Class 12 – Cultural Captain: helped organise and coordinate school events, including the Tech Fest, Model United Nations (MUN), cultural programmes and Chef Maestro.

Serving on the Student Council for two consecutive years gave me my first experience of responsibility in a team. Behind every event there is planning, and when something goes wrong, someone has to be accountable for fixing it. That is what these roles taught me.

05Competitions & Academic Engagement

Competitions & Academic Engagement

Competition · Participated

Macro Minds 3.0 – IIM Calcutta

Macro Minds 3.0 is a national-level macroeconomics competition organised by the Economics Clubs of IIM Ahmedabad (Equipoise) and IIM Calcutta. I competed as part of a four-member team against students from across the country.

In Round 1, our team cleared the macroeconomics quiz, which tested current affairs, economic concepts and market trends under time pressure. In Round 2, we prepared a structured PowerPoint presentation analysing a recent economic event, using data, policy implications and evidence-based arguments. Our team was eliminated at this stage.

Even so, the competition deepened my understanding of economic indicators, inflation, fiscal policy and financial markets, and showed me how to build an argument from data. It strengthened my interest in macroeconomic research and policy analysis.
